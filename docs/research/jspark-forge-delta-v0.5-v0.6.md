Companion to `docs/research/jspark-forge-feature-inventory.md` (the v0.4.2 baseline, F1–F78, P1–P10). This file
lists only what changed in v0.5.0, v0.5.1, v0.5.2 and v0.6.0 (released 2026-11-20 17:42 UTC → 2026-11-21 10:41 UTC),
and maps each item to the orchestrator port.

**Method.** Read-only. Sources fetched without auth:
- `GET /repos/harishgoswami23/jspark-forge/releases` (release bodies = release notes, read in full for all four).
- `GET /repos/.../compare/v0.4.2...v0.6.0`: 97 commits, 126 files (commit subjects read in full; patches read for
  `blocklist.js`, `redact.js`, `confirm.js`, `rules.js`, `setup.js`, `memory.js`, `hooks/hooks.json`, `templates/rules.md`).
- `raw.githubusercontent.com/.../v0.6.0/...` for the new and changed sources. **Read in full:** `crash.js`,
  `inflight.js`, `transcript.js`, `usage-report.js`, `tiers.js`, `fsx.js`, `rules/short-answers.md`,
  `rules/usage-care.md`, `skills/usage|waiting|check/SKILL.md`, `docs/spec-coverage.md`. **Read in the relevant
  parts:** `forge.js` (SessionStart, PreToolUse wrapper, Stop, SubagentStart, Notification, the error path, temp-file
  note), `handoff.js` (log-based history, `sinceSave`, `outsideCommit`, previous-good pair, helper texts),
  `waiting.js`, `log.js` (`hookErrors`, cap), `git.js` (file count), `graph-run.js` (gate runner), `decide.js`
  (`promote`), `artifacts.js` (header and functions), `uiskills.js` (header), `doctor.js` (check list),
  `templates/graphs/planned.md`. **Not read:** `bundled.js`, `companions.js`, `autoupdate.js`, `scripts/mcp/*`,
  `companion-install.js` beyond the release notes and commit subjects; `docs/design-v0.5.md` by headings only;
  `docs/spec.md` diff not read.
- Tests: the new/changed test files were downloaded and their top-level `test(` calls counted (same method as the
  baseline). Forge's own claim: 1,933 tests at v0.6.0 (was ~1,518 at v0.4.2), CI on 3 OSes × Node 18/22/24.

  `feat/forge-policy` (HEAD `25b250f`), plus greps of `worker.py`.

final wave), 3 = resume/handoff, 4 = decisions, 5 = workflows/gates, 6 = knowledge/doctor.
